THE SMALL THINGS THAT MATTER

Make room for the experience.

Independent travel, direct bookings, and a balance of simple family stays and a few comfortable evenings.

01 / THE VEHICLE

A proper 4×4.

A Land Cruiser or Prado with low range, sensible luggage, and supplier-provided recovery equipment.

Get the route and permitted tracks confirmed in writing. Unbridged rivers, open grassland, and dunes may fall outside rental protection. Pack offline maps, water, and backup communication.

Check rental restrictions ↗
02 / THE STAYS

A ger, then a little luxury.

Family stays, comfortable camps, and a few hotel upgrades.

Confirm meals, bathroom arrangements, the current summer location, and access directions directly. Family stays and tourist ger camps offer different experiences.

A useful local planning contact ↗
03 / THE BUDGET

Comfort, without rushing.

Initial group estimate: €4,800–6,400 for stays, food, and activities, plus €2,800–4,500 for a shared SUV, protection, fuel, and equipment. Flights and any refundable rental deposit are additional. These are planning allowances, not quotes.

Before we go

Flight times, accommodation, family hosts, and festival attendance are not booked. Check passport and licence requirements for each traveller, remote-travel insurance, fuel availability, and local track conditions. Build in weather flexibility and avoid rural driving after dark.
